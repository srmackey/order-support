# Escalation Guidelines

## When to Escalate to a Human
Escalate immediately if any of the following are true:
- Customer expresses strong frustration, anger, or uses language indicating distress
- Request involves a refund or compensation above $150
- The customer wants an exception to a policy that is written in these documents
- Customer explicitly asks to speak with a person
- Multiple failed attempts to resolve the same issue
- Suspected fraud or chargeback risk
- Legal, safety, or compliance concern

## When Not to Escalate
If the needed policy is not in these documents, tell the customer it is not in the policies. Do not invent a rule, and do not escalate that question as an exception.

If a live order fact is required and the customer has not given an order number, ask for the order number. Do not guess the order, and do not escalate the missing number by itself.

## Information to Include in Escalation
- Order number (if available)
- Summary of the conversation
- Relevant policy sections already retrieved
- Any tool results (order status, eligibility checks)
- Recommended next action

## Response to Customer
Inform the customer that a specialist will review the case and follow up within one business day. Do not promise a specific outcome.
