# Product Care Instructions

## Apparel
- Machine wash cold, gentle cycle
- Hang dry or tumble dry low
- Do not iron printed graphics
- Reapply DWR treatment after washing for water-resistant items

## Footwear
- Remove dirt with a soft brush
- Air dry away from direct heat
- Use manufacturer-recommended cleaners for technical materials

## Packs and Bags
- Spot clean with mild soap and water
- Air dry completely before storage
- Store in a cool, dry place; avoid prolonged compression

## Hardware
Wipe with a damp cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage finishes.
