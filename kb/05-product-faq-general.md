# Product FAQ — General

## What materials are your products made from?
Most apparel uses recycled polyester or organic cotton blends. Hardware is corrosion-resistant aluminum or stainless steel. Specific material details are listed on each product page.

## Are your products waterproof or water-resistant?
Many outerwear items are water-resistant (DWR finish). Fully waterproof items are explicitly labeled with a waterproof rating. Check the product description for details.

## Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes. Gift wrapping is available for $4.95 at checkout. A gift message can be included at no extra charge.

## Can I change or cancel my order?
Orders can be changed or canceled within 1 hour of placement, provided they have not yet entered processing. After that window, standard return procedures apply once the item is delivered.
