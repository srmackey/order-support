# Privacy and Data

## What information do you collect?
We collect information necessary to process orders (name, shipping address, email, payment details) and optional account information. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

## How is my payment information handled?
Payment data is processed by our PCI-compliant payment partners. We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.

## Can I request deletion of my data?
Yes. Contact support with the subject “Data Deletion Request.” We will remove personal information subject to legal retention requirements (e.g., order records for tax purposes).

## Do you use cookies?
Yes, for site functionality, analytics, and personalized recommendations. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser or via the site banner.
