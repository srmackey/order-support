# Contact and Support Hours

## How can I reach support?
- Email: support@trailforge.example
- Chat: Available on the website during business hours
- Phone: 1-800-555-0199 (business hours only)

## Support Hours
Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM Mountain Time  
Saturday, 10 AM – 2 PM Mountain Time  
Closed Sunday and major holidays

## Expected Response Times
- Chat: Typically under 5 minutes during business hours
- Email: Within 1 business day
- Escalated cases: Specialist follow-up within 1 business day

## What information should I have ready?
Order number, email address used at checkout, and a brief description of the issue.
