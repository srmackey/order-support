# Warranty Information

## Standard Warranty
All products include a 1-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

## What Is Covered
- Manufacturing defects
- Failure of components under normal use
- Stitching or hardware failure not caused by misuse

## What Is Not Covered
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from accidents, misuse, or improper care
- Modifications or repairs performed by anyone other than an authorized service center
- Loss or theft

## How to Make a Warranty Claim
Contact support with your order number and a description (and photos if possible) of the issue. We will provide instructions for return or local repair options.

## Extended Warranty
An optional 2-year extended warranty is available at checkout for most items.
