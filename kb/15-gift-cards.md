# Gift Cards and Store Credit

## Gift Cards
Gift cards are available in any amount from $25 to $500. They never expire and can be used online or in-store. Gift cards cannot be returned.

## Store Credit from Returns
When a return is processed as store credit (customer choice), the credit is applied to the account and can be used on future purchases. Store credit does not expire.

## Checking Balance
Gift card balances can be checked on the website with the card number and PIN. Store credit balances appear in the account dashboard.

## Lost Gift Cards
Contact support with proof of purchase. We can reissue the remaining balance to a new card or account credit.
