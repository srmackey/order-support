# Returns Process FAQ

## How do I start a return?
Contact support with your order number and the reason for the return. We will confirm eligibility and provide a return authorization and shipping instructions.

## Who pays for return shipping?
Customers pay return shipping for standard returns. We provide a prepaid label for defective, damaged, or incorrectly shipped items.

## How long does a refund take after you receive the return?
Inspection typically occurs within 2 business days of receipt. Refunds are then issued to the original payment method and appear within 5–7 business days.

## Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
Exchanges are processed as a return plus a new order. Once the return is received, we can prioritize the replacement shipment.
