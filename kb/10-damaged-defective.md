# Damaged or Defective Items

## What should I do if my item arrives damaged?
Contact support within 7 days of delivery. Include your order number and photos of the damage and packaging. We will arrange a replacement or refund and provide a prepaid return label if needed.

## The item stopped working after a few uses. Is it covered?
If the failure is due to a defect in materials or workmanship and falls within the warranty period, it is covered. Contact support with details and photos. Normal wear is not covered.

## I think I received the wrong item.
Contact support with your order number and a photo of what you received. We will ship the correct item and provide a return label for the incorrect one at no cost to you.
