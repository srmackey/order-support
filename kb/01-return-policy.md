# Return and Refund Policy

## Standard Return Window
Customers may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund.

## Conditions for Return
- Item must be unused, in original packaging, and in resalable condition.
- Original receipt or order number is required.
- Customer is responsible for return shipping unless the item is defective or we made an error.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Personalized or custom-engraved items
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale"
- Items that have been used outdoors or show signs of wear
- Gift cards

## Refund Timing
Once we receive and inspect the return, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Defective or Incorrect Items
If an item arrives damaged or is not what was ordered, contact support within 7 days. We will provide a prepaid return label and expedite the replacement or refund.
