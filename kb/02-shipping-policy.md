# Shipping Policy

## Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation.

## Shipping Methods and Estimated Delivery
We offer only these shipping methods:
- **Standard Ground**: 5–7 business days — $5.95 (free on orders over $75)
- **Expedited**: 2–3 business days — $12.95
- **Overnight**: Next business day (order by 1 PM local time) — $24.95

## International Shipping
We currently ship only within the United States and Canada. Canadian orders may incur additional duties and taxes at the border, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

## Order Tracking
A tracking number is emailed once the order ships. Customers can also look up status using their order number.

## Shipping Address Changes
Address changes are possible only before the order has shipped. After shipment, the carrier must be contacted directly.
