# Account and Login FAQ

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Use the “Forgot Password” link on the login page. A reset email will be sent to the address on the account. The link expires after 24 hours.

## Can I change the email address on my account?
Yes. Log in and update it under Account Settings, or contact support if you no longer have access to the original email.

## How do I view my past orders?
Log in and go to Order History. Support can also look up orders by order number or the email address used at checkout.

## Do you have a loyalty or rewards program?
Yes. Customers earn 1 point per dollar spent. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future orders. Enrollment is automatic with account creation.
