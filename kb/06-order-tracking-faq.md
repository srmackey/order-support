# Order Status and Tracking FAQ

## How do I track my order?
Use the tracking number in your shipping confirmation email, or provide your order number to support for a status lookup.

## My tracking number isn’t updating. What should I do?
Carrier scans can be delayed. If there has been no movement for more than 48 hours after the first scan, contact support with your order number.

## I received a partial shipment. Where is the rest of my order?
Some orders ship in multiple packages. Check your email for additional tracking numbers. Support can confirm whether remaining items are still pending or were canceled.

## Can I pick up my order instead of shipping?
In-store pickup is available only at our flagship location. Select “Store Pickup” at checkout if eligible.

## What comes from the order record
Order status, the delivery date, and whether an item is final-sale or personalized come from the order record. Answering those needs an order number. Do not infer them from the customer's description alone.
